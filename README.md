# 📚 StudyBuddy – User Personas & Journey Mapping

## 📌 Project Overview

StudyBuddy is a study companion concept designed to help learners plan their studies, improve learning habits, and track their progress. This project focuses on creating two illustrative user personas and mapping their journey from discovering the app to completing a study task.

The project identifies user goals, behaviors, pain points, and UX opportunities at each stage to understand how StudyBuddy can provide a simple, motivating, and user-friendly learning experience.

## ✨ Key Features

- **User Persona Development:** Created two illustrative personas representing different types of learners.
- **Persona 1 – Aisha Khan:** A high school student who wants to improve grades and stay organized with a manageable study schedule.
- **Persona 2 – Rohan Mehta:** A college student who wants to prepare for exams and manage multiple subjects while tracking progress.
- **User Journey Mapping:** Mapped six key stages of the learning experience:
  - Discovery
  - Onboarding
  - Planning
  - Learning
  - Tracking
  - Completion
- **Pain Point Identification:** Identified challenges users may face throughout their study journey.
- **UX Opportunity Analysis:** Suggested improvements such as quick sign-up, flexible study plans, focused learning sessions, reminders, personalized resources, and visual progress tracking.
- **Key UX Takeaways:** Highlighted the importance of simple onboarding, clear navigation, personalized suggestions, and celebrating milestones.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Understand different learner needs, goals, and behaviors.
- Identify potential usability challenges throughout the user journey.
- Explore opportunities to improve the study experience.
- Create a more organized, engaging, and user-friendly study companion concept.
- Apply user-centered design principles to UX research and journey mapping.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills

- User Persona Development

## 📂 Project Deliverable

A visual UX case study containing two user personas and a six-stage user journey map, including user actions, pain points, and potential UX improvements.

## 💡 Key Learnings

- Understanding user needs and motivations through personas.
- Mapping user interactions across different stages of an experience.
- Identifying pain points and translating them into UX opportunities.
- Designing around simplicity, accessibility, motivation, and progress tracking.
- Recognizing the importance of validating illustrative personas with real users where possible.

## 🚀 Conclusion

The StudyBuddy User Personas & Journey Mapping project demonstrates how understanding users and their experiences can guide better UX decisions. By identifying learner challenges and proposing practical improvements, the project establishes a foundation for designing a more supportive and engaging study companion.

*Note: The personas are illustrative concepts, not verified research findings.*
